I used to think every side project needed a tidy purpose. A portfolio piece. A startup seed. A polished little lesson.

Lately I have been trying something else: making things with no immediately defensible outcome. A type experiment that only works at one screen size. A button that makes a tiny sound. A map of cafes I might visit someday.

## The unfinished drawer

There is a folder on my computer called `maybe-later`. It is less a graveyard than a greenhouse. Ideas sit there, awkwardly alive, until some future version of me recognizes what they want to become.

The trick is to leave the door open. Not every experiment needs to graduate into a product. Sometimes it only needs to teach you a new shape.
